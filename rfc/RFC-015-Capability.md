# OSP RFC-015: Capability Protocol

**Status:** Draft
**Version:** OSP Core v1.0

## Abstract

Defines what an Actor or service is allowed and able to do. Capability is separate from identity.

## Capability Examples
- create_post
- search
- translate
- moderate
- generate_media
- execute_task
- access_memory

## Grants
Capabilities MAY be granted, limited by scope, time, resource, or Country policy, and revoked.

## AI
AI is not a special identity class. AI behavior is represented through capabilities, permissions, metadata, and events.
