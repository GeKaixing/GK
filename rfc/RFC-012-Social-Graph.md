# OSP RFC-012: Social Graph Protocol

**Status:** Draft
**Version:** OSP Core v1.0

## Abstract

Defines portable social relationships between Actors.

## Relationship Types
Examples:
- follow
- friend
- block
- mute
- subscribe
- trust
- collaborate

## Ownership
The Social Graph is Actor data and should be exportable and migratable.

## Cross-Country Relationships
An Actor in Country A MAY maintain a relationship with an Actor in Country B, subject to recognition, Customs, and permission rules.

## Implementation Status

**Draft — reference implementation landed in Gekaixing v3.2.0 (cross-country social graph: follow, like, reply between countries).**
